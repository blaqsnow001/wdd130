Featured Trips

Family Float Adventure

Duration: Half Day | Difficulty: Class I-II | Ages: 5+

Perfect for families and first-time rafters! This gentle journey down the scenic Lower Canyon offers calm waters, beautiful scenery, and plenty of opportunities to spot wildlife. Our experienced guides will share fascinating stories about the area's history and ecology while ensuring everyone has a safe and enjoyable experience.

Highlights: Wildlife viewing, swimming stops, picnic lunch included

Starting at $79 per person

Thunder Rapids Expedition

Duration: Full Day | Difficulty: Class III | Ages: 12+

Experience the thrill of moderate whitewater rafting on our most popular trip! Navigate exciting Class III rapids with names like "The Washing Machine" and "Rocky's Revenge." This full-day adventure includes a riverside lunch, multiple rapid runs, and opportunities to swim in calm pools between the action.

Highlights: Exciting rapids, riverside gourmet lunch, professional photos included

Starting at $149 per person

Extreme Wilderness Challenge

Duration: 2 Days | Difficulty: Class IV | Ages: 16+

For experienced rafters seeking the ultimate adrenaline rush! This two-day expedition through the wild Upper Canyon features powerful Class IV rapids, remote wilderness camping, and breathtaking canyon scenery. This trip requires excellent physical fitness and previous rafting experience. Limited availability.

Highlights: Class IV rapids, wilderness camping, all meals included, shuttle service

Starting at $399 per person
